# Aviator Game Testing Guide

Welcome, testers! This guide will help you test the Aviator game and ensure it's working perfectly. Your feedback is crucial, so thank you for your help!

## How to Test

The main goal is to play the game as any normal user would and see if anything looks strange or broken. Pay attention to the game's visuals, animations, and numbers.

### 1. Playing the Game

*   **Objective:** Make sure the basic game works smoothly.
    *   **What to do:**
        1.  Start the game.
        2.  Place a bet before the round begins.
        3.  Watch the plane fly and the multiplier go up.
        4.  Click the "Cashout" button before the plane flies away.
    *   **What to look for:**
        *   Did your balance update correctly after you won?
        *   If you didn't cash out in time, did you lose your bet?
        *   Can you cancel a bet before the round starts? Does your balance update correctly after cancelling?

### 2. Multiplayer Testing

*   **Objective:** Ensure the game is the same for all players.
    *   **What to do:**
        1.  Have multiple people play the game at the same time on different devices.
        2.  Everyone should watch the game at the same time.
    *   **What to look for:**
        *   Does the multiplier number look exactly the same on all devices?
        *   Does the plane fly and crash at the exact same time for everyone?
        *   When a player bets or cashes out, does it show up correctly in the "All Bets" list for everyone?

### 3. UI and Animations

*   **Objective:** Check for any visual glitches or weird animations.
    *   **What to look for:**
        *   Do all the buttons work correctly?
        *   Do the animations (like the plane flying, the multiplier increasing, and the background effects) look smooth?
        *   Do any UI elements overlap or look out of place?
        *   Switch between different apps on your phone and then come back to the game. Does everything still look and work correctly?

### 4. Analytics and Game History

*   **Objective:** Make sure the game's data and history are being recorded and displayed correctly.
    *   **How it works:** The game keeps track of a lot of data. The "Analytics" section shows you this data in different ways.
        *   **Previous Rounds:** This shows the results of the last few rounds, including the final crash point and who won what.
        *   **Top Rounds:** This is a leaderboard that shows the biggest wins. You can filter it to see the highest multipliers ("x") or the largest winnings ("wins"). You can also filter by time (day, month, year).
    *   **What to do:**
        1.  Play a few rounds of the game.
        2.  Go to the "Previous Rounds" section.
        3.  Go to the "Top Rounds" section and try out the different filters.
    *   **What to look for:**
        *   Does the "Previous Rounds" section show the correct results for the rounds you just played?
        *   Does the "Top Rounds" section update correctly with new high scores?
        *   Do the filters in "Top Rounds" work as expected? For example, if you filter by "wins", does it show the players who won the most money?

Thank you for your time and effort in testing the game!
